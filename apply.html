Thank You for Everything

After a lot of thought, we’ve made the difficult decision to close Kairos Meal Preps.

We are incredibly grateful to every customer, client, athlete, business, and organization that trusted us over the years. What started as an idea grew into something much bigger because of the people who supported us, ordered meals, referred friends, and allowed us to be part of their lives.

Thank you for supporting a local business and for being part of our journey.
